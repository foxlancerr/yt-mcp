# Copyright & clean-clip rules (every short)
Each short lives in `clips/<idea-name>/` (source.md, raw/, edit/, final.mp4, caption.md, check.md).
1. Permission first: campaign-supplied/allowed source, official asset used per its terms, or written creator OK. Proof in source.md. No permission = no clip.
2. No logos or watermarks (channel, stream overlay, platform, brand marks).
3. No ads, sponsor reads, promo codes.
4. No copyrighted music.
5. No third-party voice unless permitted.
6. Transformative: own hook/commentary, short, never raw re-uploads.
7. Credit in caption text only.
8. No leaks, piracy, spoilers, nudity, misleading "official" wording.
9. Human approves every post; nothing auto-uploads.
