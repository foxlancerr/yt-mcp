## 3. The edits

Every plan below is a **9:16 vertical, 20–42 s** cut. Timecodes are output time. `src` is the source
stream clock. Camera vocabulary is fixed and reused:

- **PUNCH-IN 120%** — hard scale jump on the cut, no animation
- **SLOW PUSH 100→112%** — linear zoom over the stated frames
- **TRACKING CROP** — reframe to follow the subject, keyframed
- **WHIP** — 2-frame directional motion blur used as a cut
- **FREEZE + settle** — hold frame, then 6% zoom settle over 4 frames
- **RAMP 100→40→100** — speed ramp, ease both ends
- **SHAKE 3px/4f** — impact shake
- **REACTION CUT** — cut to facecam, hold ≤ 12 frames
- **J-CUT** — audio of the next shot leads the picture by 4–8 frames
