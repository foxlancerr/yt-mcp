## 1. Why this works at 12 seconds

Your three lines are already a **stated contrast** — the strongest hook shape in the
skill library, and it fits in 11 seconds:

| Line | Shape | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unfair judgment | setup — viewer recognises it instantly |
| 2 | Opposite judgment | reversal — "so nothing I do is right?" |
| 3 | Dismissive punchline | payoff — stop arguing with people, just build |

The only structural weakness: **line 1 is a setup, not a hook.** It states a fact
the viewer already knows. At 12 seconds you cannot afford 3.3 seconds of setup.

Two fixes, pick one:

- **Fix A (recommended, keeps your timings):** line 1 stays but the **first frame
  already shows the judgment happening** — a silhouette pointing at empty pockets.
  The visual is the hook, the line is the confirmation.
- **Fix B (higher risk):** re-cut so line 3 lands first ("بہرحال، پیسے کما لو") and
  lines 1–2 play as the flashback. Punchline-first works on Shorts but breaks your
  SRT and reads as a different video.

Everything below assumes **Fix A**.

## 5. Text-on-screen specs (Urdu is RTL — get this right)

- **Font:** Noto Nastaliq Urdu (or Jameel Noori Nastaleeq). Do **not** use a Latin
  font for Urdu — the letters break.
- **Alignment:** right-aligned, right-to-left. Line breaks at natural pauses, never
  mid-word.
- **Safe area:** keep text inside the middle 80% — Shorts UI covers the bottom ~15%
  (title/CTA) and the right ~10% (buttons).
- **Size:** 5–7% of frame height per line. Max 3 words per line.
- **Colour:** cream `#F5F5DC` normally; red `#FF0000` only on the two judgment words
  (`کچھ نہیں کرتے`, `مغرور`).
- **Do not** add a background box. The black void already gives contrast.
- **Burn in**, do not use auto-captions — YouTube's Urdu ASR mangles Nastaliq badly
  and would ship wrong text in your own language.
