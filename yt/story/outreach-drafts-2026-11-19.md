### The one thing I will not write

You asked for comments that "make the audience come to my channel". There is no
version of "check out my channel" / "I make similar videos" / a link that survives —
that is solicitation, it gets the comment deleted, and repeated attempts restrict the
account's ability to comment at all. The pull has to come from the comment being good
enough that someone clicks your name. That is what these are built for: each one is a
person who clearly watched the video and had a thought worth replying to.
