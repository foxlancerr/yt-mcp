## 0. The honest constraint that shapes this script

GTA 6 is only live in New Zealand as of 11:00 UTC 2026-11-18. There is no
legitimate GTA 6 footage library, and other people's clips are a copyright strike
waiting to happen. Rockstar's policy allows **your own gameplay**, non-commercial,
no spoilers, no leaked footage.

So this script is built in two layers:

- **Layer A — capture** (you record in-game after your region unlocks)
- **Layer B — animate** (The Runner 2D, for beats the game may not let you stage)

The narration never claims these are real game events. It is Lucia's story, told
over capture where capture exists.

## 3. Capture spec

- Resolution: 1080p60 minimum, PS5 Share / Xbox capture; record 3–4× longer than needed
- **Mute the radio.** GTA radio is licensed music — a guaranteed claim. All audio is your VO + sound design.
- Shoot for a 9:16 crop: keep Lucia in the vertical third, avoid HUD in the crop zone
- No cutscenes cut together as isolated blocks (Rockstar removes isolated cinematics)
- No graphic violence in the first 7 seconds (YouTube ad-eligibility, Sept 2026 rules)
- Log every clip with its timecode; you'll need ~40s of usable coverage per 2 min

## 9. Hook architecture — the curiosity map

| Time | Device | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Tone line** — "Vice City doesn't stop for anyone. Not even for her." | Establishes the world and that *she* is the exception, in 11 words |
| 0:04 | **Specificity** — eleven dollars, a stolen car, forty minutes | Three concrete numbers buy credibility the animation didn't earn yet |
| 0:13 | **Inciting turn** — the lights come on | The promise of the title is delivered 13 seconds in, not 60 |
| 0:22 | **Re-hook** — "She doesn't run. That's the part nobody expects." | Breaks the chase-scene template the viewer predicted |
| 0:42–0:55 | **Clue loop** — the guard isn't watching her, he's watching the door | One detail, two lines apart, unanswered for 13 seconds |
| 1:13 | **Motive reveal** — the bank is still lit | Answers "where is she going" without answering "why" |
| 1:39 | **Character flip** — she smiles | The victim becomes the author; this is the line people quote |
| 1:44 | **Open loop** — "The first shot isn't hers." | Cuts on a question — the only honest way to end episode 1 |

**Why the hook works:** the title promises an arrest, the first line promises a
world that doesn't wait, and the second line gives numbers instead of adjectives.
The viewer stays for the *gap between "arrested" and "walked out"* — which is
never explained until 0:55, and then only as a smell and a door.

**If you want a colder open** (test against the current line):
- "Lucia gets arrested 90 seconds into her own story. Then the guard looks away." (14)
