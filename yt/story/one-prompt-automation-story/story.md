(beat) The machine buys you time. Taste is still the toll.

…the queue.

upload. Private. Scheduled for twenty hundred UTC —
America, Britain, Germany, Australia — one hour.
Playlist. Comment. Analytics. The loop closes.
(→) Which brings us to the part nobody shows you.

### [3:25–3:55 — SCENE 6: THE LOOP — the hidden truth]
The machine does not make millions.
No system does.
It makes time. Ten thousand units a day. Fifty credits. Six uploads.
And the creators who win aren't the ones who automate the most…
they're the ones who automate the boring part,
and spend the saved hours being different.
(beat) The wave doesn't check your subscriber count.
It checks your patience.
