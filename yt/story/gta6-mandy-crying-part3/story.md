It is a cross-posted clip, which is why it surfaced on Facebook rather than in a Speed stream. **Speed never plays this moment** — his Mandy window ends at 2:05:30 on `qSyo5d-dVzA`, before the tears.

### The dialogue — verified, not paraphrased

Cross-checked against **five independent playthroughs** (`FJOXuas-OBg`, `k3esjgmbphQ`, `akMuwfhCOQg`, `TRLa9vENmUQ`, `gxqLAHK0EnE`) plus GamesDB's character transcript. All five agree, word for word:

> **JASON:** "What the hell are you even crying about?"
> **MANDY:** "What goes around comes around, you know. **You'll get yours, Jason.**"
> **JASON:** "All right, well that sounds like a you problem, Mandy."
> **MANDY:** "Just go, Jason. Oh, I need time, Jason."

**This is the whole story.** She is crying, he is bored, and she promises him something. He calls it a *you problem*. The game then spends the next hour showing you what she is capable of.

### Why this beats the "sad Mandy" framing

Every clip of this scene is cut as **comedy** — "Jason broke her heart 😭😂", "coldest line ever". The creator's own description laughs at it. Nobody cuts it as **the warning it is**.

The line *"You'll get yours, Jason"* is not a breakup line. It is a threat, and the house backs it up:

| Clue | What is there | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The sink | What Jason pulls out **appears to be a severed finger**; she snatches it back instantly | IGN, GameSpot, gta6.house (20 Nov) |
| The trunk | Meat cleaver, duct tape, battery acid, loppers, rope | deep-dive transcript `0A0kddg_T18` |
| The yard | A filled-in **body-sized hole**, acid tubs, bleach, a shovel | IGN, GameSpot, gta6.house |
| Her text | *"If you weren't so hot, I'd chop all your fingers off so you'd go crazy…"* | transcript |
| The photo | A framed picture of **Jason, Mandy and Cal at the Rusty Anchor** — a memory she built alone | transcript |

**And there is no affair.** Polygon (18 Nov) and every guide confirm Jason stays faithful to Lucia and the game never lets you romance Mandy. The relationship exists only in her head. That is what makes the threat land.

**Runtime 60.0s / 17 shots → one visual change every 3.53s.** No shot exceeds 4.0s. The two cards that run 4.0s each carry an internal change (second line lands 2.0s in), and the loop card animates continuously — so nothing sits static.

**Never cut these short (narrative rule):**
1. `"What goes around comes around, you know. You'll get yours, Jason."` — **the whole payload.** Cutting before "you'll get yours" destroys the video.
2. `"All right, well that sounds like a you problem, Mandy."` — the dismissal is what makes the threat scary. It must land complete and dry.
3. `"Just go, Jason. Oh, I need time, Jason."` — the exit that makes the later text believable.

**Trim order if it must be shorter:** S15 (photo) → S10 (trunk) → S7 (exit). Never touch S2, S5, S6, S12, S13.

**The re-hear at 0:41 is the engine of this cut.** The viewer hears "you'll get yours" at 0:11 as a bitter ex-line. After the finger, the trunk and the yard, the *same words* play again at 0:41 and mean something completely different. That is the retention trick — no new footage, no new information, just a changed listener.
