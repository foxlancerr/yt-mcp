## 1. What each setting actually does

Labels are from the captures. Behaviour is verified where a source exists, marked as
inference where it does not. This table is the honesty spine of the video.

| Tab | Setting | Default shown | What it actually does | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AUDIO | **Mute Licensed Music** | **Off** | Removes licensed tracks (radio, club, cutscene songs) from the mix. Industry-standard streamer switch. | ✅ high |
| DISPLAY | **Dismemberment** | **On** | Removes limb loss / gore on hits when off. | ✅ high (label) |
| DISPLAY | **Motion Blur Strength** | **3** | Camera-motion blur amount. 0 = no smear on fast turns. | ✅ high (label) |
| GAMEPLAY | **Movement Speed** | **Walk 1/4** | Out-of-combat locomotion preset. Walk is the slowest of four. | ✅ high (UI) |
| GAMEPLAY | **Combat Movement Speed** | **Fast Walk 2/3** | Strafe/locomotion speed while fighting. Two of three is not the top. | ✅ high (UI) |
| GAMEPLAY | **Shooting Assist** | **On** | Aim assistance. Rockstar's previews describe GTA 6 as dropping lock-on for "a little bit of stickiness", tunable to nothing (Rob Nelson, Kinda Funny preview). | ✅ high (preview-sourced) |
| GAMEPLAY | **Drive-By Assist** | On | Aim help while firing from a vehicle. | ⚠️ inferred from label |
| GAMEPLAY | **Quick Draw** | On | Faster weapon raise on draw. | ⚠️ inferred |
| GAMEPLAY | **Holster in Vehicle** | Automatic | Auto-holsters when entering a car. | ⚠️ inferred |
| GAMEPLAY | **Cover Selection** | Camera & Movement | Whether cover snapping follows camera direction or stick movement. | ⚠️ inferred |
| GAMEPLAY | **Stand & Sprint** | Off | Sprint activation behaviour. **Could not verify from any source.** | ❌ unknown |
| DISPLAY | **Awareness Effects Intensity** | 5 | Likely the peripheral/wanted-state visual effect. Not documented anywhere. | ❌ unknown |
| AUDIO | **Vehicle Radio** | Default 1/3 | Which station loads by default when you enter a car. | ⚠️ inferred |

**The thesis of the video:** this menu is not a comfort page. It is a **creator
toolkit and a difficulty slider wearing comfort labels** — and the two settings that
matter most are both off / on by default in the direction that hurts you.
