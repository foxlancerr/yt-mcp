## Paid lane (campaigns)

Snapshot 2026-11-22: 53 live campaigns mention GTA 6, ~$143K left, typical $1.00/1K, top $3.00.
Only 19 are streamer-clipping briefs; 26 are music/edit briefs and 3 are logo briefs. Re-read
`clipcpm.com/gta-6` before cutting — budgets drain hourly.

- **Never use the brand channel.** Reach asks for a dedicated account (`LACYClips` style) and
  polices audience geography and account niche.
- **Pools are a dilution trap here.** Effective rates at read: Lacy $0.14/1K on 89.4M views,
  Kai Cenat $0.26, Clix $0.47 — against $1.00–$2.00 per-view briefs. Skip them.
- **The streamer's name is the hook.** The top GTA campaign clip was "DID HE JUST CRACK LACY??"
  at 21.5M views. Name in the first two words; hashtag exactly as written.
- **Cut to the cap, not to fame.** At $2.00/1K with a $150 cap the ceiling is 75,000 views; ten
  clips beat one banger.
- **Original audio only.** RUCKUS bans AI voiceover and translated subtitles; Reach bans
  AI-generated video with a permanent ban. Ported from the briefs, not a preference.
