## 🔍 Winning Patterns

- **Topics:** the streamer's own past words coming true, endurance records, saves lost, bugs, rankings, and "is it actually good" arguments.
- **Titles:**
  - `[STREAMER] Said [CLAIM]. [TIME] Later…`
  - `[STREAMER] Said He'd Be [AGE] Before [GAME] Came Out`
  - `DON'T [DO THE OBVIOUS THING] — Ranking [THING]`
  - `[STREAMER] Says [GAME] Isn't [EXPECTATION]`
  - `[STREAMER] [VERB] [ABSURD OBJECT] in GTA 6`
- **Hooks:** claim in frame one, so the title is already being paid off at 0:00. **Contradiction in bold**, face in the upper third, timer or number overlay.
- **Thumbnails:** 9:16, streamer face centre, **2–4 words maximum**, one number or timer, blurred gameplay behind. Every one of the six viewed does this.
- **Length:** 13–48s wins. Median winning clip is ~30s.
- **Triggers:** schadenfreude (Speed failing), parasocial loyalty (Speed vs Kai vs Jynxzi), spoiler-hunting (endings), and **taking a side** (the only reliable comment engine).
- **For your channel:** views and engagement pull in opposite directions here. Clipeon's 1.68M clip has **0.05 comments per 1,000 views**; Sammynibro's 1.86M has **0.03**. Feed viewers swipe, they do not talk. The opinion clips run 20–80× the comment density on a fraction of the views. Build the video so it gets the clip's reach and the opinion's argument.
