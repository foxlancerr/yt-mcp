## 6. What not to do

- **Don't enter last-to-leave.** 0.1–1.4× across the board; the format belongs to 7M-sub channels.
- **Don't cut down P1/P2 into Shorts as a growth lane.** Our own 15s average confirms it.
- **Don't claim GTA 6 gameplay, leaks, or "I played it".** Review copies are watermarked; leaks are a copyright and trust risk, and we have no footage.
- **Don't chase the 350M MrBeast ceiling.** Ratio, not ceiling, is our metric.
- **Don't publish "free code" content as a promise.** Twitch drops are chance-based; the wording must say so.
