## 4. The five problems, ranked by cost

### P0 — The launch window is allocated wrong
Today's six GTA uploads went out **07:00–12:13 UTC**. At that moment:
- NZ had been live 73 minutes (that's where the Lucia capture came from)
- AU unlocked at 13:00 UTC — **47 minutes after** the last upload
- UK unlocks 00:00 Nov 19 · US 05:00 Nov 19

So the "launch" videos were published **12–18 hours before your two largest
markets could press start**. By the time UK/US search traffic peaks, those videos
are a day old and already buried in the channel page.

Meanwhile the Nov 19 slot holds a hidden-location mystery at 20:00 — decent, but
15 hours after US unlock, and not the thing people will be searching for.

### P1 — Burst publishing into a 0-subscriber channel
11 uploads in 36 hours (5 on Nov 17, 6 on Nov 18 by noon), on top of a queue
already running one per day. The cost isn't the feed test — each Short gets its
own — it's that:
- **YT_CHANNEL is 16% of traffic (9 views).** Those people land on a page showing
  six uploads in five hours, several about the same launch hour. It reads as a
  content farm, and the session ends.
- There is no binge path. A viewer who finds one video has nothing next to watch.
