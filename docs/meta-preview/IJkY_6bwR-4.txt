WE ESCAPED AN ANCIENT ARMY… BUT THEY FOUND US (Part 4)

THE KING'S OWN GENERAL BETRAYED HIM… AND NOW HIS ARMY IS HUNTING US! 😱

Four friends. One ancient kingdom. One dangerous secret.

Our time travel survival adventure continues in ancient Mesopotamia, around 2000 BCE. After discovering a mysterious record of our arrival in the royal archive, the king finally reveals the truth: the army hunting us no longer serves him. His most trusted commander, General Zukar, has betrayed the throne and wants to take control of the kingdom.

But Zukar isn't just after the crown. He is searching for the ancient royal seal — the key to a secret hidden inside the Temple of the First Dawn.

The king gives us the seal and sends us on a dangerous mission. While the city falls into chaos, we must get through a crowded execution courtyard, escape across the rooftops of ancient Uruk and reach the enormous temple before Zukar's soldiers catch us.

Will we discover what the ancient seal unlocks? Why are four strangers from the future connected to the kingdom's fate? And what does General Zukar really want?

The closer we get to the temple, the more dangerous the mystery becomes. And just when we think we've made it, Zukar arrives with an army.
