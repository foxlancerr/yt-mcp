Why You Keep Starting but Never Finish Anything

Why you keep starting but never finish — the psychology of procrastination, and the four forces that decide whether you follow through.

Four forces influence the way you think, work, and live:

Motivation makes you start, but can disappear when things get difficult.
Ego make you chase approval and prove yourself to others.
Obsession pushes you forward, sometimes making you forget what you're sacrificing.
Discipline keeps you moving, even when you don't feel motivated and nobody is watching.

But which one is actually controlling your life?
