I WOKE UP IN A JUNGLE FULL OF ZOMBIES! 🧟🌴

I opened my eyes… and realized I was hanging high above a jungle packed with zombies. I had no idea how I got there, how long I had been asleep, or why something was climbing the tree beneath me. One wrong move could send me straight into the horde.

This was supposed to be a normal day. Instead, I woke up in the middle of a nightmare.

With only a small backpack, a few broken branches, and nowhere safe to go, I had to figure out how to get off the giant tree before the zombies reached me. But the jungle had more surprises waiting.

A broken rope bridge stood between me and the next platform. As I tried to escape, a zombie grabbed my backpack. I barely managed to break free, but the bridge started collapsing beneath my feet. Then the support rope snapped, leaving me hanging above hundreds of zombies.

Just when I thought things couldn't get worse, something much bigger appeared.

A giant mutated zombie emerged from the trees and reached for the same vine I was using to survive. I spotted a rescue helicopter through the fog. It was my only chance. I jumped, grabbed the ladder, and finally started rising above the jungle.

But was I really safe?

As the helicopter climbed higher, the giant zombie made one final move… and grabbed the aircraft.

Now I'm trapped between the sky and a jungle full of zombies. And I have no idea what happens next.

Will I escape, or will the zombies pull me back down?
