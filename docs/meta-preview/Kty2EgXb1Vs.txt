After the helicopter brought me to an abandoned research facility, a mysterious voice called my name: “We've been waiting for you, The Untold Exit.” Before I could understand, a hand reached out of the darkness, grabbed my hoodie and pulled me inside. The steel door slammed shut.

Then the facility called me SUBJECT 07.

A warning echoed through the building: something had followed me from the jungle. Outside, hundreds of zombies rushed toward the entrance. Inside, the facility began locking every corridor. I ran as a security shutter came crashing down, slid underneath it, and barely escaped. But the infected creatures suddenly stopped. Instead of chasing me, they turned toward a huge glass wall. Something enormous struck it from the other side.

I escaped into a control room, where an old recording started playing. On the screen, I saw myself lying unconscious on a laboratory table. A scientist's voice said: “Subject 07. Memory reset successful. Release him into the jungle.”

I had been here before.

Then the screen revealed an even more disturbing message: OUTBREAK SOURCE: SUBJECT 07. The facility warned that I must not reach containment. A blue-lit laboratory door unlocked, leading me into a room filled with glass chambers.

Inside one chamber, someone was waiting.

It was ME.

Another The Untold Exit. Same face. Same hair. But he looked exhausted and terrified. He slammed his hands against the glass and screamed: “DON'T LET THEM OPEN THE DOOR!”

Then he told me something I never expected:

“THE ZOMBIES WEREN'T HUNTING YOU… THEY WERE KEEPING YOU OUT!”

I looked at the security monitor. Outside, the zombie horde was no longer attacking the building. They were backing away from it.

Suddenly, the facility announced:

“SUBJECT 07 HAS RETURNED. BEGIN RELEASE OF SUBJECT 08.”

The chamber locks began opening. The other The Untold Exit screamed, “NO! NOT AGAIN!”

Who is Subject 08? Why does the facility have another version of me? And if the zombies were protecting me, what is really inside this building?
