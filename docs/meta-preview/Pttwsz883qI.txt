Why Do You Get Exhausted? Burnt Out.

Introverts and extroverts can walk into the same party, enjoy the same people, and walk away feeling completely different.
One wants the night to continue. The other can't wait for a little quiet.

But does that mean one enjoys people more than the other?

Meet Alex and Ryan. Through one party, you'll discover how introversion and extraversion shape social experiences, why enjoying people can still leave you exhausted, and why needing quiet doesn't mean you're unhappy. We'll explore what psychological research actually says about personality, social connection, and everyday happiness. Because maybe neither person is experiencing the night incorrectly.

If you've ever felt like Alex or Ryan, leave a like, share your experience in the comments, and subscribe for more questions worth thinking about.
