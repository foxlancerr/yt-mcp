### 3.10 The operating context in `AGENTS.md` is now contradicted by the data

§3 says "longer narrative + survival content holds attention; sub-1-min cutdowns do not", citing 261s average view duration on the Jungle video and 180s on Underwater. Those numbers came from videos with **1 to 5 lifetime views**. They are single-viewer artifacts, not retention.

The current data says the opposite: the 300-second NVIDIA episodes have **3 and 5 views**; the 454-second jungle video has **1**; the 141-second volcano is unpublished. Meanwhile 16-second Shorts are earning 643–1,191 views at 54–85% completion.

The correct conclusion is not "long-form is bad" — it is **"long-form cannot cold-start on this channel yet."** There is no browse traffic for a 4-sub account; Shorts are the only distribution surface that will show a stranger your video. Long-form becomes viable *after* Shorts build the audience, not instead of them. That reframing matters because the current plan schedules long-form into a feed that will not show it.

### 3.11 Monetization is not close, and the working lane is the one with the risk

| Route | Requirement | Current | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long-form | 4,000 watch hours / 365d | 29.4 h per 28d | ~3,800 days at this rate |
| Shorts | 10M Shorts views / 90d | ~18k per 90d | ~555× |
| Either | 1,000 subscribers | 4 | at 0.05% conversion ≈ 2M views |

So: don't plan around monetization; plan around proving **one** format can repeatedly clear 10k views. That is the only milestone that changes the shape of the curve.

And the uncomfortable part: the format that is working is **borrowed footage** — iShowSpeed clips and captured GTA 6 gameplay. Rockstar gameplay capture is widely tolerated but claimable; streamer footage is the higher-risk half, and you have already accepted that risk deliberately. The relevant exposure at monetization review is not copyright alone: heavy reuse of another creator's footage without meaningful original transformation is a standard rejection reason at YPP review. The current Mandy/Jason packages already solve this by design (2D cards for anything without footage, our own narration, our own cut points) — that is exactly the right instinct and it should be a hard rule, not a fallback.
