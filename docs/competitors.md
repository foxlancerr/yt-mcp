## 🧭 Moves

- **Copy:** Long-form 8–15 min, one repeatable title template iterated weekly,
  daily-to-every-other-day cadence, and turning any video that outperforms ≥5×
  median into a multi-part series within 72 hours.
- **Avoid:** Shorts as a growth engine — cluster shorts run 0.05–1.4× sub ratio
  and The Untold Exit's own cutdowns (P6, 15s avg on Spot the Millionaire)
  already prove it. Sub-1-min cutdowns are a dead format here.
- **Positioning:** The mystery-brand voice — where Miko/ConChaser hand the viewer
  a power fantasy, The Untold Exit hands them a question to solve ("the door only
  opens for one person — here's why"). Same engine, differentiated promise.

## Anti-patterns observed (do not copy)

- **Topic misses inside winning templates:** Miko's "Birthday Powers" (2.7K),
  "Declared Dead" (5.1K), "Island of 100 Amazons" (10K) vs its truth-revealing
  hits (165K–933K). The *seeing hidden truths* family wins; random powers don't.
- **Title drift:** You Variable's lowercase "part02" wasted a slot; every title
  must carry the full curiosity frame every time.
- **Cadence collapse:** channels that slowed (Dart's Aug→Oct gap) needed a
  template reboot to recover.
