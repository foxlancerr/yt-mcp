**The source clip**

- [Kai Cenat Finds Out Lucia Is Cheating On Him 💔](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9OnZGMWDQ88) — Kai Cenat Live's own channel, 0:10, Nov 20 · **824,545 views**, 11,632 likes

**The problem: it is factually false.** Six outlets independently confirmed there is no infidelity mechanic — [Beebom](https://beebom.com/does-lucia-cheat-on-jason-in-gta-6/), [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-romance-options-cheat-on-lucia-mandy/), [Radio Times](https://www.radiotimes.com/technology/gaming/gta-6-can-jason-lucia-cheat-on-each-other/), [Output Lag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-lucia-relationship/), [The Gamers Hub](https://thegamershub.co/news/gta-6-did-jason-cheat-on-lucia-mandy-pipework/), games.gg.

**The four "proof" clips, debunked**

| Clip | What it looks like | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Speed scrolling Lucia's phone | Texts cancelling plans, messages stop delivering | Pre-prison breakup flashback; she went inside after the split |
| Lucia in a pink hoodie beside a shirtless man | "Cheater" | No guide or article names the man; the word was added by the clipper |
| A message ending "nonsexual snuggle buddy" | Read as "sensual" | Cal Hampton's joke about bunking with Jason |
| "Confession" that she lied about the baby | Cheating confession | *Coming Home*: Lucia tells Jason **he is Tomas's father** — the opposite |

The real red herring: Chapter 4, *Making Gainz* — Lucia gets close to bodybuilder **Nate** at Ajax Gym, but it is an undercover crew job **Jason is in on**, set up with the photo trap in *Dirty Work*.

**The play:** a debunk, not a repost. It is the only version of this topic that survives YouTube's metadata-accuracy rules, and it is literally the channel tagline — *Look Beyond What Seems Obvious*.

## ⚠️ Policy gate (checked before writing)

- **No third-party footage without a licence or permission** — IShowSpeed/Kai clips are not CC BY; reaction-only framing is defensible but not a licence. Flagged, not cleared.
- **Metadata accuracy** — a title claiming a streamer did something he did not do violates YouTube's deceptive-practices policy. The "Kai saved Lucia's mom" claim is unverified and was rejected on that basis.
- **Spoilers** — endings content needs the spoiler frame in the title, not a fake claim.
