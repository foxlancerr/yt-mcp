## The answer in one line

The hook type that wins is **not** suspense. It is a **plain statement plus a number and a
negation** — "the game never tells you", "5 things I didn't know" — paid off within 3 seconds.
Nothing is withheld. The curiosity comes from *the count* and *the gap*, not from teasing.

Every top video's first line is a flat sentence. No adjectives, no "you won't believe".

## The five archetypes, ranked

### 1. Numbered information gap — highest ceiling
`[N] Things You Didn't Know About GTA 6` · `GTA 6 Tips The Game Never Tells You`

- **Proof:** 10M views (35×) and 478K views on 944 subs.
- **Why it works:** the number is a promise of finite length, the "didn't know / never tells
  you" is the gap. Item one lands in the first 3 seconds, so the viewer gets paid immediately
  and stays for the order.
- **Copy rule:** count must be 5–10, said out loud in the first line, then `Number one…`
  straight away. Never "stick around to see number five".

### 2. Capability shock — highest velocity
`You Can Do ALL This In GTA 6?!` · `I Played GTA 6 For 5 Hours. Here's What's In It.`

- **Proof:** 2.9M views at **192K views/hour** — the fastest-spreading video in the sample.
- **Why it works:** a time anchor ("I played for five hours") buys credibility for an
  abundance claim, and abundance reads as generosity rather than a tease.
- **Copy rule:** the anchor is real and specific (hours played), the claim is a single "and
  there's more than you think".

### 3. Urgency checklist — best for a small channel
`Do These 10 Things Before You Start GTA 6` · `Do This Before Playing`

- **Proof:** 478K on 944 subs; thumbnail is a red/yellow checklist with a "NO SPOILERS" badge.
- **Why it works:** it implies a **cost of missing out**, which is stronger than curiosity,
  and it is not spoiler-risky — the viewer can act right now.

### 4. Contradiction — highest multiplier, needs an expectation
`GTA 6 Running The Way It Should Have On Release` · `No, GTA 6 Is Not GTA 5 With Better Graphics`

- **Proof:** 144× on 6.1K subs; the vidIQ title-change data shows a channel going from
  23.9 to 91.9 views/hour (×3.8) by switching *"GTA 6 Heists Will Be NOTHING Like GTA 5"*
  to *"No, GTA 6 is not GTA 5 with better graphics"*.
- **Why it works:** it takes a side against a claim the audience already heard.
- **Copy rule:** the contradiction must name something the audience believes *right now*.
  Without a live belief it is just a negative title and dies.

### 5. Opinion / taking a side — the comment engine, not the view engine
`GTA 6 Isn't Living Up To The Hype` (from the 2026-11-21 streamer scan)

- **Proof:** only 297K views, but **3.93 comments per 1,000 views** — 20–80× the clip
  channels' 0.03–0.05.
- **Use it for:** comments and return viewers, never for reach.

### What dies
- **Vague mystery without a payoff** — "what nobody noticed" with no item one.
- **Streamer-name keywords without an event** — `gta 6 rage` median 93K vs
  `ishowspeed gta 6` 1.6M (2026-11-21 scan).
- **Cutdowns of our own long-form** — our own P6 data: 15s average view.

## Thumbnail system (from the actual images)

Two working systems. Both are readable at 120px wide, neither uses a face cam.

**A — one-word verdict over gameplay.** JayWayy: the single word `UNDERESTIMATED` in heavy
white caps across a raw gameplay frame. The word is the argument; the frame is evidence.

**B — checklist + imperative + safety badge.** Loredonia: `DO THIS BEFORE PLAYING!` in
yellow/white over a red burst, a small `10 TIPS ✅ / NO SPOILERS ✅` panel, a red arrow, the
GTA VI logo, and a character posed on a landmark. Busy, but every element answers "should I
click".

**Spoiler-safe badges are a filter, not a hook.** During launch week people avoid spoilers
more than they chase tips. "NO SPOILERS" removes the reason to scroll past.

## The copy template for The Untold Exit

Keep our mystery brand, strip the purple prose. Data says plain wins.

**Title (30–40 chars, curiosity + count or negation):**
- `5 Things GTA 6 Never Explains` (30)
- `GTA 6 Punishes This. Nobody Warns You.` (37)
- `I Played 10 Hours. It Never Explained This.` (41)

**First line (must contain the count and the gap):**
> "In GTA 6, no spoilers — but there are five things the game never explains."

**Then item one immediately.** No intro, no channel housekeeping, no "before we start".

**Structure:** 5 items × 5–7s, item one at 0:03, each item = claim + proof clip. 45–60s total.

# Three filmable scripts

All three are in the winning archetypes. Gameplay facts are `[FILL]` — **you** verify them
in your own capture. I will not invent GTA 6 mechanics, and a wrong tip in the first 10
seconds kills the video and the channel's credibility with it.

## Script 1 — `5 Things GTA 6 Never Explains` (archetype 1 · 50s)

- **Thumbnail:** word `UNEXPLAINED` over a gameplay frame; 2-word plate `NEVER TOLD`.
- **VO 0:00–0:04:** "In GTA 6, no spoilers — but there are five things the game never explains."
- **1 (0:04):** [FILL: mechanic the tutorial skips — verify in-game] · show it, don't describe it.
- **2 (0:14):** [FILL]
- **3 (0:24):** [FILL]
- **4 (0:34):** [FILL]
- **5 (0:44):** [FILL] — the one with the biggest consequence.
- **Close:** "Number five is the one that costs you the save." (no CTA, no subscribe ask)

## Script 2 — `I Played GTA 6 For 10 Hours. It Never Told Me This.` (archetype 2 · 55s)

- **Thumbnail:** word `10 HOURS` + small `STILL LEARNING`.
- **VO 0:00–0:05:** "I played GTA 6 for ten hours straight. Here's what it never told me."
- **Beats:** three discoveries in ascending strangeness, each a visible clip + one sentence.
  [FILL ×3 — only things you actually found]
- **Close:** "Ten hours in, and the game still hasn't explained it."

## Script 3 — `Do These 5 Things Before Chapter 2` (archetype 3 · 45s)

- **Thumbnail:** checklist panel `5 THINGS` + `NO SPOILERS` badge, red arrow.
- **VO 0:00–0:04:** "Five things to do in GTA 6 before Chapter 2 — no spoilers."
- **Items:** [FILL ×5 — each must be a missable with a real consequence; verify the miss is
  permanent before claiming it is]
- **Close:** "After Chapter 2, they're gone." **Only** if you verified they are gone.

## Policy gate (manual check, AGENTS.md §4.0)

- **Footage:** none of the sampled videos may be re-cut. Their gameplay is their copyright.
  Every script above assumes **our own capture**. Without it, the video cannot ship.
- **Metadata accuracy:** every number in a title ("5 things", "10 hours") must be true of
  the video. No count inflation.
- **Spoilers:** the "no spoilers" promise must be kept in the cut, not just the thumbnail.
- **No fake claims:** no invented mechanics in exchange for a stronger hook.
- **Brand:** The Untold Exit only; "OhBhai!" retired.
