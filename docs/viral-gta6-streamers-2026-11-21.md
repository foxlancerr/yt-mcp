## What the data shows

1. **The biggest numbers are 4-24 hour live streams.** They cannot be reused: every upload above is a standard YouTube license, not Creative Commons.
2. **The best ratios come from tiny clip channels making 20-60s Shorts about the streams:**
   - Recap Shorts Clips: 3.22M views on 1,480 subs (2,176x), 21s, "ISHOWSPEED DOESN'T KNOW IF GTA 6 IS ON PS5 OR XBOX 😭💀".
   - zuxa: 1.40M on 922 subs, 28s, "Speed Throws Kid Over in GTA6 💀💀".
   - Insomnia Clipz: 2.78M on 6.1K subs, 32s, "Kai Cenat's GTA 6 Reaction Went WRONG 😭".
   - Lobby de Arquivos: 842K on 2,470 subs, 65s, best moments of three Brazilian streamers.
3. **Title pattern in those Shorts:** [named person or character] + [reaction or fail] + 1-2 emotion emojis (😭💀). The emotion sits in the title, the payoff in the first seconds.
4. Edited long videos (Champs, Dattrax) win on gameplay spectacle (5-star chase, first download), not on a face.
5. Ratio is views divided by subscribers. It shows reach, not retention or revenue.
